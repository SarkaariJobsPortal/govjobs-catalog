This notice explains what the app does — and, mostly, does not do — with information about you.

In short: your profile, preferences, and saved jobs never leave your phone. The only thing this app fetches from the internet is a public list of job postings — it does not send your data anywhere to get it.

1

Information you enter

During setup, you tell the app your age, state, highest education level, degree or stream, and experience, and you may add your first name (optional, used only to greet you). You also rank the sectors and job types you're interested in, and choose where you want to see openings from.

All of this is saved only in the app's private storage on your own device (Android's DataStore). It is never uploaded, transmitted, or made visible to the developer or anyone else.

2

Jobs you save

Jobs you bookmark, mark as applied, hide, or open are remembered in a small local database on your device, the same way, so the app can stop showing you things you have already dealt with. You can delete everything at any time from Me → Privacy and your data → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android's app settings, also removes it permanently — there is no copy anywhere else to delete, and Android cloud backup is switched off for this app.

3

Where job listings come from

The app downloads a single, pre-compiled file of current government job postings over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. This is a one-way, anonymous request for public data — it does not include your profile, preferences, or any device identifier, and the developer has no record of who made it or when.

4

What the app does not do

No account or sign-in is required. The app requests two Android permissions — internet access, to fetch the job list above, and permission to show notifications, which Android asks you about only when you turn on the optional daily digest. It asks for nothing about location, contacts, camera, or storage. It contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software of any kind, so nothing about your usage is measured or transmitted.

The daily digest is a single notification a day, worked out on your phone from the job list and your saved jobs. No server is involved, and you can turn it off in Me → Notifications.

no accounts no location access no advertising no analytics or tracking no data sold or shared
5

Sharing and disclosure

Because nothing personal is collected in the first place, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose — to advertisers, analytics providers, or anyone else. This may change only if a future version of the app adds a feature that requires it, in which case this notice will be updated first.

6

Accuracy of job listings

Listings are gathered from public government sources and job-listing sites for your convenience, and are not independently verified against official eligibility rules. Always confirm details — deadlines especially — on the recruiting organisation's own notification before applying.

7

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

8

Changes to this notice

If this app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised accordingly. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated notice.

9

Contact
